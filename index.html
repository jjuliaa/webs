Computational Evolution of Sensing & Cognition

Life self-organizes and evolves on the basis of molecular and information-based dynamics. Natural environments are high-dimensional and contain potentially infinite kinds of useful signals, and yet the sensory systems of organisms each become attuned to highly specific, relevant ranges of signals that they efficiently represent.

Developmental processes produce chemical gradients that can store and provide positional information across the cells of the embryo. Both of these processes also evolve and change over a lifetime and over generations of experience, where different molecular mechanisms may serve similar informational purposes.

I study what might be shared across different information dynamics, from molecules to minds, to understand how different kinds of complex cognition evolve. Are there more primitive building blocks of complex cognition that are repurposed across scales?
